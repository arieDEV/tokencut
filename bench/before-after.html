Cara membedakan hasil uji

Tanpa fork

Dengan fork

Checklist cepat: (1) apakah file sumber muncul penuh di chat utama? (2) apakah ada manifest.json + PROMPT.md? (3) apakah jawaban datang dari worker low?

Catatan

  • Token counts are approximate (bytes/4), not a provider billing meter.
  • Worker still processes the full corpus on the low tier; savings are mainly on the expensive main chat.
  • This is not Spotify's 90% AiKA figure — that needs Portal + AiKA pricing.
  • Quality depends on the worker answer; spot-check line numbers before editing.
